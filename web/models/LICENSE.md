classification heads from the Essentia models collection, by the Music
Technology Group, Universitat Pompeu Fabra:
https://essentia.upf.edu/models.html

Citation:
Alonso-Jiménez, P., Serra, X., & Bogdanov, D. (2022). Music Representation
Learning Based on Editorial Metadata from Discogs. ISMIR.
