          Mood map

          Each track by brightness and intensity, joined in the order played. The cross is this set's own middle.

          Brightness: the mood model's happy score, plus a little for major keys. Intensity: energy, with the aggressive and party scores. Both are relative to this set, because a phone in a loud room pulls every score toward the middle.
